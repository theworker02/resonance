# Fireworks Detector

Distinguishes fireworks from gunfire and other impulsive events.

## Key discriminators

- **Aerial burst**: sharp crack + long reverberant tail with secondary pops
- **Ground level**: lower energy, more mid-range frequency content, shorter tail
- **Sustained display**: repeated bursts at irregular 0.3–8 s intervals

Fireworks differ from gunfire primarily in their spectral tail complexity and
the presence of secondary pops (shell fragments).
