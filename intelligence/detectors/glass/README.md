# Glass Break Detector

Classifies glass breaking by type: residential window, commercial plate glass,
and vehicle (tempered) glass.

## Key discriminators

- **High-frequency ringing**: all glass types exhibit this above 4 kHz
- **Ring duration**: residential > commercial > vehicle (tempered shatters quickly)
- **Low-frequency content**: commercial plate glass has higher mass → more LF energy
- **Spectral flatness**: vehicle glass (tempered) tends toward more broadband shatter
