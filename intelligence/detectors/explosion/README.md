# Large Explosion Detector

Detects large-scale explosion events: industrial accidents, structural demolition,
and large ordnance. Distinguished from the impulsive detector by requiring higher
peak energy thresholds and longer reverb tails.

## Key discriminators

- **Industrial**: gradual pressure rise, long tail, strong low-frequency component
- **Structural**: multi-phase envelope (primary + secondary detonations)
- **Ordnance**: fastest attack (< 15 ms), highest spectral flatness, longest tail
